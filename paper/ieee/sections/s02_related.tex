\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Mandi and Indian agricultural price forecasting.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Paul et al.\ compared ARIMA, GRNN, SVR, random forests and gradient boosting on daily AGMARKNET brinjal prices from 17 Odisha markets, with Diebold--Mariano tests \cite{paul2022brinjal}.
  \item A study of monthly prices for 19 commodities in 143 markets found deep recurrent models ahead of others, and included a random-walk-with-drift baseline \cite{paul2025deep}.
  \item Hybrid, wavelet and neural approaches exist for oilseeds, pulses, onion and potato \cite{paul2021wavelet,jha2012tdnn,jha2013ann,anjoy2017pulses,kumar2021onion,praveenkumar2024potato,jaiswal2021lstm}.
  \item The closest study in coverage uses daily AGMARKNET data for 23 commodities in 165 markets with an 80/20 split, and explicitly decides against MASE \cite{manogna2025deep}.
  \item AGMARKNET data quality, including missing and erroneous entries, has been addressed in price-forecasting frameworks \cite{jain2020agmarknet,guo2020agmarknet}.
  \item Combination methods for agricultural commodity prices have appeared in this journal \cite{wang2022bee}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
Our protocol follows established recommendations:
\begin{itemize}
  \item scaled errors \cite{hyndman2006another};
  \item tests of equal predictive accuracy \cite{diebold1995comparing};
  \item strong simple benchmarks \cite{makridakis2018statistical,makridakis2020m4,makridakis2022m5};
  \item time-series performance estimation \cite{bergmeir2018note,cerqueira2020evaluating};
  \item the catalogue of pitfalls in machine-learning forecasting \cite{hewamalage2022pitfalls}.
\end{itemize}
The consistency of point forecasts with the scoring function \cite{gneiting2011making} is particularly relevant to intermittent and zero-inflated targets \cite{kolassa2016evaluating}.
Real-time data vintages are known to change forecast evaluations in macroeconomics \cite{bokun2023fredsd}. Our provenance finding is an agricultural analogue, associated with an incomplete mirror rather than with revisions.

\paragraph{Uncertainty.}
Conformal methods for time series include EnbPI \cite{xu2023conformal}, ensemble conformalised quantile regression \cite{jensen2024encqr} and adaptive conformal inference \cite{gibbs2021adaptive}.
A drift-aware conformal method has recently been applied to potato prices in China \cite{ouyang2026potato}. We use plain split conformal with Mondrian volatility bins and \emph{measure} its coverage.

\paragraph{Information and farmers.}
Some information interventions have raised producer prices \cite{jensen2007digital,goyal2010information}; others had small or no average effects \cite{fafchamps2012sms,mitra2018asymmetric}. These results motivate cautious decision support.

\paragraph{Gap.}
To our knowledge, no study of Indian mandi prices combines all four of the following:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item national-scale daily series;
  \item purged chronological evaluation against naive, seasonal and per-series statistical baselines at several horizons;
  \item measured interval coverage;
  \item an audit of the data source against the official record together with prospective scoring.
\end{enumerate}
